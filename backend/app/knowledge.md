App: TradeWise (stock trading and investing app)
- Open account: verify mobile OTP, add PAN, Aadhaar, bank details, e-sign and selfie. Active in 1-2 working days.
- Brokerage: delivery trades low or zero brokerage; intraday and F&O small flat fee per order; taxes extra. Table in Profile > Pricing.
- Add funds: Funds > Add money via UPI or net banking. Withdraw: Funds > Withdraw to linked bank, usually 1 working day.
- First order: search stock, tap Buy, choose quantity, Market or Limit, confirm. Track in Orders tab.
- KYC pending or rejected: usually PAN and Aadhaar name mismatch, blurry document, or unlinked mobile. See Profile > KYC status.
- Failed or pending payments: pending ones usually clear or refund in 1-2 working days.
